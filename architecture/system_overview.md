# Compliance Copilot AI - System Overview

## Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to understand legal obligations, identify applicable regulations, maintain statutory compliance, and manage frequent regulatory updates. Legal professionals spend significant time reviewing documents, mapping regulations, and preparing compliance reports manually.

---

## Proposed Solution

Compliance Copilot AI is an Agentic AI-based legal compliance platform that automates business consultation, document understanding, compliance assessment, and recommendation generation while keeping lawyers in the decision loop through a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) architecture.

---

## Users

### Client
- New Business
- Existing Business

### Lawyer
- Reviews AI-generated recommendations
- Approves or modifies legal advice

## AI Agents

| Agent | Responsibility |
|--------|----------------|
| Business Consultation Agent | Collect business information |
| Business Profiling Agent | Build organization profile |
| Registration Planning Agent | Recommend registrations |
| Document Intelligence Agent | Extract document information |
| Regulation Mapping Agent | Map regulations |
| Compliance Audit Agent | Evaluate compliance |
| Citation Verification Agent | Validate legal references |
| Risk Prediction Agent | Predict compliance risks |
| Recommendation Agent | Generate recommendations |
